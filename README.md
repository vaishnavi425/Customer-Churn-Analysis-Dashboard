# Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns to identify high-risk customer segments and understand the key factors associated with customer churn.

The project uses MySQL for data exploration, cleaning, and business analysis, and Power BI to build an interactive six-page dashboard for visualizing customer behavior, churn patterns, and business insights.

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates
- Understand the impact of contract type, internet service, payment method, and tenure on churn
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides an overview of customer churn and retention performance through key KPIs and summary visualizations.

### 2. Customer Insights
Analyzes customer demographics and behavioral characteristics such as gender, senior citizen status, dependents, and partner status.

### 3. Service & Contract Analysis
Analyzes churn patterns across contract types, internet services, payment methods, online security, technical support, and online backup.

### 4. Churn Risk Analysis
Identifies customer segments and behavioral patterns associated with higher churn risk based on tenure, monthly charges, and total charges.

### 5. Churn Rate Analysis
Compares churn rates across contract types, internet services, payment methods, customer tenure, and monthly charge groups.

### 6. Business Insights & Recommendations
Summarizes key findings and provides actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

## Key Business Insights

- Month-to-month customers show the highest churn rate, indicating lower customer commitment.
- Fiber optic customers have a relatively higher churn rate compared with other internet service categories.
- Electronic check users demonstrate the highest churn rate among payment methods.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn, highlighting the importance of early-stage retention strategies.
- Customers with higher monthly charges generally show a higher risk of churn.

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve Early Customer Retention
   - Introduce onboarding programs and engagement campaigns for new customers.

2. Target Month-to-Month Customers
   - Offer incentives to encourage customers to move toward one-year or two-year contracts.

3. Investigate Fiber Optic Churn
   - Analyze service quality, pricing, and customer support issues among fiber optic users.

4. Review Electronic Check Payments
   - Investigate whether payment experience or billing issues contribute to higher churn.

5. Introduce Proactive Retention Campaigns
   - Identify high-risk customers and offer personalized discounts or service benefits before they churn.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights key customer segments associated with higher churn risk. The findings can help businesses develop targeted retention strategies, improve customer experience, and reduce customer churn.
